How lead sources work

1. Add a sourceGoogle Sheet link, uploaded CSV or Excel file, or a file on this computer.
2. Choose a tabIn a campaign, under Leads → Lead source.
3. Match columnsCompany, website and contact columns are matched automatically. Missing columns are listed.
4. Write resultsGoogle Sheets: results go into the pipeline columns only. Files: results stay in the app and can be exported.
Pipeline columns: website_status · email_status · icebreaker_status · push_status · reply_status. They are added only after you confirm. Other columns are never changed.

Website research

Step 1. Reads each company website and stores the facts used for ice breakers.

How it works

1. Check the domainUnreachable domains are marked and skipped.
2. Read key pagesHome, About, Services, Careers and News, up to 5 pages.
3. Extract factsOpen roles, awards, sectors served, software partnerships, case studies.
4. StoreFacts are saved in the app. The sheet receives website_status only.

Results

Replies

Step 6. New replies are collected every 30 minutes and classified. Select a reply to open it.

How replies are handled

1. CollectNew replies from the sending platform, every 30 minutes.
2. ClassifyInterested, not interested, out of office, wrong person or unsubscribe.
3. Recordreply_status is written to the sheet, so the lead is not contacted again by mistake.
4. NotifyInterested replies appear on Home under Needs your decision.
